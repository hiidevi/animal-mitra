About Animal Mitra

Animal Mitra is Faridabad's comprehensive directory of animal rescue organizations, NGOs, and individual helpers. We connect people who need help for animals in distress with verified rescue organizations and volunteers. Whether you've found an injured street dog, need help with cattle, or want to report animal abuse, we provide you with accurate contact information and resources to get immediate help.

Our Mission: To ensure no animal in Faridabad goes without help by making rescue resources easily accessible to everyone.
