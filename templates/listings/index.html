🐾 About Animal Mitra

We Don't Rescue - We CONNECT

Animal Mitra is NOT a rescue organization. We are Faridabad's trusted information directory that connects people who need help for animals in distress with those who CAN help - whether you're an NGO, individual helper, Instagram animal lover, or just someone who cares.

We share information. You make the difference.

How It Works

1️⃣
Register on Platform

Anyone can register - NGOs, individuals, Instagram animal helpers, veterinarians, or simply animal lovers. No certification needed!

2️⃣
People Find You

When someone nearby needs help for an animal, they search our directory and find helpers in their location. They can call, WhatsApp, or message you directly.

3️⃣
You Decide to Help

It's YOUR choice! No pressure, no obligation. Help when you can, in the way you can - rescue, financial support, shelter, or just advice.
