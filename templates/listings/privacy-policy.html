Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Animal Mitra. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, location
  • Profile Information: Information you provide when registering as a helper or NGO
  • Usage Data: How you interact with our website
  • Cookies: We use cookies to improve your experience (see Cookie Policy below)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Connect people with animal rescue helpers
  • Verify helper and NGO accounts
  • Send important notifications
  • Improve our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We only share your information when:

  • You explicitly make it public (e.g., your helper profile)
  • Required by law
  • Necessary to provide our services

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Update: Correct your information
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from emails

6. Cookie Policy

We use cookies to:

  • Remember your preferences
  • Keep you logged in
  • Analyze website traffic

You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie consent popup.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Secure password hashing
  • Regular security audits

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

10. Contact Us
